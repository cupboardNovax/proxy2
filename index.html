Student Practice Center

Standards-aligned practice for Kindergarten through Grade 12. Explore learning across core subjects, world languages, and test preparation.

Mathematics

Build skills from counting, place value, and fractions through algebra, geometry, precalculus, statistics, and calculus. Practice includes worked explanations for topics such as equations, logarithms, trigonometry, limits, derivatives, and integrals.

English Language Arts

Develop reading comprehension, close reading, main idea, author's purpose, and evidence-based analysis. Writing practice covers narrative, expository, argumentative, and research writing, along with grammar and punctuation.

Science

Explore habitats, matter, forces, cells, heredity, ecosystems, plate tectonics, chemistry, physics, and environmental science. Practice scientific thinking through hypotheses, variables, data tables, graphing, and error analysis.

Social Studies

Study United States and world history, civics, geography, and economics—from ancient civilizations and the Constitution to modern history, federalism, supply and demand, and personal financial literacy.

World Languages

Explore Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, and English as a Second Language through dialogue, vocabulary, pronunciation, and writing practice.

Test Preparation

Prepare for the SAT, ACT, PSAT, AP examinations, state assessments, and language certifications including DELE, DELF, Goethe-Zertifikat, HSK, JLPT, TOPIK, TOEFL, and IELTS.

How Practice Works

A short diagnostic helps identify a starting skill level. Questions adapt as accuracy improves, missed skills return for spaced review, and explanations walk through the reasoning behind each answer.

Accessibility

The described learning experience supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, adjustable text size, high contrast, extended or untimed practice, and text-to-speech for reading passages.
